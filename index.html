Common questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

We quote a fixed price for the job after hearing the problem. Emergency out-of-hours visits are priced clearly on the phone before we set off.

How quickly can you come out?

Most weekday calls are seen the same or next day. For leaks and no-heating emergencies we aim to be with you as soon as we can.

Is my new boiler guaranteed?

Yes. New installs come with the manufacturer's guarantee, plus our own workmanship cover.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card, bank transfer or cash on completion. Larger installs can be split into stage payments.
